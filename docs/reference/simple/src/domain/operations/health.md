# Foundation health

Vision turns synchronization freshness, authorization, failures, capacity warnings, and AI spend into one of four states. Losing Google authorization always wins, followed by failures that need action, delays, and finally healthy.

## `calculateFoundationHealth`

Calculates the visible state, sync and queue ages, AI tier, and safe warning codes.

## `classifyAiSpend`

Maps the exact $8.00, $9.00, and $9.50 boundaries to display tiers.

## `validateFoundationHealthFacts`

Rejects malformed counters or misleading timestamps.

## `validDate`

Checks that a status timestamp is real and not in the future.
